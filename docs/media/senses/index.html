Human sense organs

Rule. A row is a distinct biological structure or afferent class that transduces a physical quantity into a signal the nervous system can process. Under it, the list has 38 rows. The count depends on the rule: 15 discrete organs, 26 everyday senses after merging sensors that serve one sense, 34 outside the brain. No source we could find says 27.

Afferent nerves and the conscious column are standard anatomy and our synthesis, not checked per citation. Much of the mechanism evidence comes from animals; the human column says whether direct human evidence was read.
